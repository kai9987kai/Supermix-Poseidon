## What changes the direction

The strongest common lesson is to measure the value of acquired information before permitting it to alter control. Poseidon already has Helm residual-history critics, TRIDENT transition controls, persistent memory, named-event randomness and durable jobs. Its research ORACLE also already implements persistent episode-bootstrap membership. Rebuilding those under another name would add little.

The proposed addition is a learned retriever trained on the *incremental decision value* of historical evidence, integrated with one artifact-bound predictor and tested on frozen structural transfer families. Correct, erased, shuffled and stale evidence must cause distinguishable predictions or actions before an outcome assay can substantiate memory utility. Source mechanisms motivate the experiments; none of the portfolio results establishes that this synthesis will improve Poseidon.

Both Expanse repositories retain composite model/data restrictions, including BioMedLM-derived OpenRAIL-M, Qwen-derived Apache-2.0 and connectome CC BY 4.0 obligations. Use independently written mechanisms and Poseidon-generated synthetic trajectories; any later imported artifact needs its own provenance and terms.
