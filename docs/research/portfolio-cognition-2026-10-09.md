## Three highest-leverage integrations

1. **Fit a compact history-conditioned residual predictor before expanding planning.** Start with a fixed 4–8-step observation/action/reward window and a regularized linear readout; then test a matched 32–64-unit bounded recurrent feature map. Inputs may include only already observed transitions and the residual from the previous completed transition. Predict action-specific next-observation residuals and H-step reward advantages, with separate horizons 1, 4 and 16. Tidal's present optional hidden carry is not a trained sequence model, so switching it on is insufficient. Freeze the incumbent and fit a separate artifact from disjoint episodes. Compare current observation-only retrieval, fixed window, persistent recurrence and state-reset recurrence; report episode return, adverse overrides, actual H16 advantage error, latency and state size. Keep a no-change action path until held-out decision evidence supports activation. This combines the comparator mechanism in Morpheus/Ghost, persistence controls in Expanse-v2 and the inexpensive recurrent control reference in Genesis.

2. **Make retrieval and navigation conditional on executable, supported decisions.** For every retrieved trajectory, bind action, horizon, frozen continuation identity, episode identity and observable resource regime. Apply exact eligibility filters before any distance/rank fusion; show support count and disagreement rather than treating ranks as calibrated advantages. For Odyssey, learn transition outcome distributions from observed macro-action transitions and reject unobserved directed steps; the existing BFS path does not itself command a destination. Forecast resource replenishment from repeated visits and measured elapsed ticks, with uncertainty when visits are sparse, instead of assuming a fixed rate is established by observations. Evaluate a small bounded set of actual macro-action candidates under physiological reserve budgets. The simulator supplies concrete energy/rest/resource controls; Nexus supplies an exact deterministic retrieval reference. Ablate action filters, regime filters and replenishment estimates separately.

3. **Require causal memory and independent candidate evidence.** Add correct-history, erased-history, time-shifted-history, state-transplanted and noise/cell-dropout analogues at identical episode anchors. Separate prediction benefit from action benefit and from generic state perturbation. Use fixed fit/selection/calibration/final-test partitions; retain moderate and scarcity anchor suites and independent unseen regimes. Compare a proposed controller to policy with matched seeds and measured compute, collecting all six actual H-step branches at every override. Morpheus's fresh-suite adoption and Prometheus's selective swaps provide implementation patterns, but reproducing an online-testing theorem requires its own assumptions and outcome definition. A controller that predicts better but yields adverse overrides remains experimental; a calibrated controller with zero overrides demonstrates deferral, not superiority. Hash the selected candidate and freeze it before final testing.

## Recent primary research considered

The following sources refine the proposals; they do not establish that any implementation here will outperform Poseidon's current controller.

- **Memory baseline:** Jeen, Bewley and Cullen, [Zero-Shot Reinforcement Learning Under Partial Observability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.15446), RLC 2025. The study considers hidden state, reward and changing dynamics, and reports benefits from memory architectures over memory-free counterparts. Its tasks and pretraining differ from TidePool. The inference for Poseidon is to measure a small history baseline before importing a specialized architecture. An established comparison is Ni, Eysenbach and Salakhutdinov, [Recurrent Model-Free RL Can Be a Strong Baseline for Many POMDPs](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v162/ni22a.html), ICML 2022.
- **Multi-step uncertainty:** Li, Krause and Hutter, [Uncertainty-Aware Robotic World Model Makes Offline Model-Based Reinforcement Learning Work on Real Robots](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.16680v3), revised January 2026. It combines temporally consistent autoregressive world modeling with epistemic uncertainty and uncertainty-penalized policy training. Poseidon can test bootstrap disagreement across small episode-fitted predictors and measure actual horizon errors; adopting the full robot-training pipeline is outside this CPU-sized proposal.
- **Planning cost:** Deb, Wright and Banerjee, [Inference Time Policy Optimization for Offline RL with Differentiable World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.22430v2), revised May 2026. It reports inference adaptation benefits in continuous-control benchmarks while identifying rollout/backpropagation cost and studying a cheaper sampler. For Poseidon's six discrete actions, this supports explicitly budgeting short candidate rollouts and measuring latency. It does not justify unbounded online gradients or transferring their numerical benchmark gains.
