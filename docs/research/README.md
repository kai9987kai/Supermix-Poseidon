The choice is one measurable mechanism with controls, rather than collecting architecture labels. TRIDENT implements observed-edge admission and identity-specific residual ablations, not a learned transition model. Remaining proposals are staged research, not implemented capabilities: calibrated transition-outcome models, full cross-carrier factorials, learned probe scheduling, symbolic template admission and decoded sprite export.

The [NEXUS-Ω decision-rank audit](nexus-omega-decision-rank-audit-2026-10-13.md) extends the same evidence-first approach to active-inference action scores. It exposes every candidate action's predicted reward and state, then compares score orderings with exact same-state simulator branches. The contextual residual adapter passed a different one-step forecast gate but increased immediate regret and vital-state error on two fresh behavior-anchor blocks; it remains opt-in. The experiment borrows the portfolio's matched intervention and inspectable ranking lessons without importing upstream code or treating those projects' synthetic results as Poseidon evidence.

The [phaseworld robustness and schedule-shift study](nexus-omega-phaseworld-v2-robustness-2026-10-14.md) adds a versioned sustainable simulator, pulse-age inference, and a resource-aware phase guard. In a fresh 64-seed, 128-step five-of-six stress block, the aligned profile survived 64/64 episodes at each of two initializations with exact replay of all 576 traces; a source-bound repeat matched every episode. An eight-of-ten schedule also survived 64/64, with minimum health 0.60. A paired two-pulse detector reduced interval-estimation lag from 26.45 to 16.45 steps on an eight-to-ten interval shift, without changing actions in that particular block. For a ten-by-six to eight-by-seven joint shift, a matched 64-seed threshold ablation raised mean reward by 7.80 (95% CI 7.67–7.93), preserved 128/128 survival and zero storm forages, and reduced guard overrides by 21.6%; minimum energy and hydration fell to 0.75 and 0.65. Results remain synthetic, bounded, and non-promotional.

The [alias-world v2 memory-value study](alias-world-v2-memory-value-2026-10-14.md) corrects the original DV-GEM pilot interpretation, adds a viable versioned resource world, and compares retained, erased, and inverted cues on fresh paired seeds. An explicit cue-memory rule improved reward over a reactive balancer in all four conditions. Training on paired counterfactual action advantage with a predictive resource guard and risk-adaptive balance margin raised survival to 100% and beat erased memory (+6.03 ID, +5.45 noise, +0.40 scarcity, +0.28 combined stress), matched absolute models, and reactive controls across 1,792 exact replays. Combined-stress minima reached 0.20 energy and 0.18 hydration; because hydration fell short of the 0.20 gate and trailed cue memory in ID/noise, it remains strictly unpromoted.

## Recent primary research

The cutoff is **9 October 2026**. Publication status and source versions matter; none of the following papers' reported scores or guarantees are Poseidon results.

- [Zero-Shot Reinforcement Learning under Partial Observability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.15446), RLC 2025, motivates testing history when observations omit state, reward or dynamics. Helm compares independently fitted history and observation-only families.
- [Future-Aware Recall](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34677), submitted 28 September 2026, is a recent preprint on training memory retrieval using later predictive usefulness. Helm uses future return labels during offline fitting; inference has only past/current observations. It does not implement FAR's video model or reproduce its evaluations.
- [DeepSPI: Reinforcement Learning in the Policy's Neighborhood for Safe Policy Improvement](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2026/hash/0212b0dff7b4aa3bd8b8cc4ce24fd384-Abstract-Conference.html), ICLR 2026, motivates limiting policy changes around an incumbent. Helm's descriptive error radii do not satisfy or inherit that paper's guarantees.
- [Uncertainty-Aware Robotic World Model Makes Offline Model-Based Reinforcement Learning Work on Real Robots](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.16680v3), revised January 2026, motivates checking temporal uncertainty instead of equating short-step fit with long-horizon control. Poseidon remains a synthetic environment, without robot validation.
- [Inference-Time Policy Optimization for Offline Reinforcement Learning with Differentiable World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.22430v2), revised May 2026, highlights the planning-cost trade-off. Helm uses a small fitted readout and records compute rather than adding unbounded online optimization.
- [SelectiveNet](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/geifman19a.html) and [Deep RL at the Edge of the Statistical Precipice](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.13264) motivate descriptive benefit/harm versus intervention coverage and episode-level uncertainty. Sequential policy safety is a different claim; zero overrides mean no exposure, not proved successful intervention.
